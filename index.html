Important distinction

This is an AI-assisted shortlist, not a background eBay scanner. eBay saved-search alerts notify you, and you add candidates here. AI cannot confirm a refractor from weak photos.

What to look for

Rainbow color splitting, reflective design areas, poor seller identification, original photos, and plausible set/year. Don't mistake a chrome base card or scanner glare for a refractor.
